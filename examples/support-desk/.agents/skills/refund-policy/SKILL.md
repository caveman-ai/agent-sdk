# Refund policy

- Full refund within 30 days of delivery, item unused with tags on.
- 31–90 days: store credit only, same condition rules.
- Used gear that failed on the trail is a **warranty** case, not a
  refund — follow the warranty rules in the standing instructions and explain
  replacement eligibility instead.
- Refund goes to the original payment method, 5–10 business days after the
  return is accepted.
- Shipping costs are refunded only when the return is our fault
  (wrong item, defect, carrier loss).

## Read-only response

State amount, method, restocking note, reason, and exact account-flow next
step. Never claim a refund was submitted or completed; this agent has no
write tool or action-request channel.

## Edge cases

- Partial refunds for missing accessories: price the missing part from
  the order record, never from memory.
- Sale items follow the same windows; "final sale" items (marked in
  the order record) are store-credit only inside 30 days.
- If the customer already opened a chargeback, stop: escalate to a
  support lead and do not recommend a refund on top of it.
