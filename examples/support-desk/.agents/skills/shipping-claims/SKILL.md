# Shipping claims

## Late

- "Late" means past the carrier estimate on the order record, not past
  the customer's hope. Quote the estimate and label it as the
  carrier's.
- Inside the estimate: reassure, share tracking, do not offer credit.
- Past the estimate by 3+ business days: explain shipping-refund eligibility
  and its account-flow next step while keeping the order moving.

## Lost

- Carrier shows no movement for 7 business days → treat as lost.
- Recommend the replacement path rather than a refund first; most customers
  want the gear, not the money back.
- If the customer prefers a refund, explain that account-flow path instead.

## Damaged

- Ask for a photo only if the customer hasn't described the damage;
  never make a photo a condition for starting the claim.
- Explain replacement eligibility for the damaged item; the customer keeps or
  recycles the damaged one — no return label for damaged goods.

## Never

- Never blame the carrier in the reply. We chose the carrier.
- Never promise a specific delivery date for a replacement — carrier
  estimate only.
